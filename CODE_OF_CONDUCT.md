# Code of Conduct

Juured is built for collaborative nonprofit hackathon work. Contributors are expected to keep
discussion respectful, practical, and focused on the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Give actionable feedback and explain technical tradeoffs.
- Assume good intent, but address harmful behavior quickly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions or reviews.
- Any behavior that makes collaboration unsafe for other contributors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or take other reasonable action
to protect the project. Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or through the repository owner.
